  • Straight time: first 8 hours worked in a day (normal shift 10pm–6am).
  • Overtime (1.5×): hours past 8 in a day, plus all of Saturday from 6am through the Saturday 10pm–6am night shift.
  • Double time (2×): Sunday 6am through Monday 10pm.
  • Weekend hours don't count toward the 8-hour overtime limit.
  • A shift belongs to the date it starts on.
  • Weeks run Monday–Sunday (by shift start). Each week is paid the Friday after it ends.
